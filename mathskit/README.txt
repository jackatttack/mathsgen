mathskit: maths lessons for Toolbox

Lessons to teach from on a shared screen. Each topic is a ladder of stages:
the idea, a worked example you step through, questions with the answer
hidden until you reveal it, Your move where it fits, and the topic's key
facts. The Bank page draws questions from the MathsGen engine in ../core.

Open it from Toolbox: the MathsGen installer adds a Maths card when
Toolbox (github.com/jackatttack/editorkit) is installed. Or run
maths_tool.py by hand.

KaTeX is bundled in katex/ under its MIT licence (katex/LICENSE).
